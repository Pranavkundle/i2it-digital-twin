An institute with one industrialist's name still on the founding plaque.

I²IT was founded in 2003 by Shri P. P. Chhabria, founder chairman of the Finolex Group, as a campus built specifically for engineering and information technology — not a general university that added a tech department later. It was dedicated to the nation by Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, then President of India, on 28 May 2003.

The campus sits inside the Rajiv Gandhi Infotech Park in Hinjawadi, in the middle of the same IT corridor where most graduates go on to work — which shortens the distance between a lab assignment and an internship considerably.

Three branches, one academic block.

All three are four-year, full-time B.E. programmes with curricula designed in-house by I²IT's own faculty, rather than handed down unchanged from a parent university.

01

Computer Engineering

Systems, software and the theory underneath both. The core degree for students headed toward development and research roles.

02

Information Technology

Networks, data and the applications layer on top of them — for students who want to ship products, not only study how they work.

03

Electronics & Telecommunication

Circuits, signal processing and embedded systems, for students headed toward hardware and communications rather than pure software.

Recruiters that have hired from campus include Infosys, Oracle 3DPLM, Accolite and BookMyShow, through the Training and Placement Cell based in the academic block.

What's on campus, beyond the classroom.

Everything below is one of the buildings in the model at the top of this page — click its marker there to jump straight to it.

Central Library

Reading rooms, journal stacks and a digital catalogue that backs up whatever's being built in the labs next door. Wifi reaches every seat.

Hostel Mithila & Hostel Nalanda

On-campus residences for women (Mithila) and men (Nalanda), with shared and single rooms, LAN in every room, and a warden on site.

Auditorium

Orientation, tech talks and placement drives happen here — the one room that seats the whole cohort at once.

Sports & Fitness Center

An outdoor track, courts for the usual indoor games, and a gym, for the hours that aren't spent in a lab.

Cafeteria

The canteen — and, unofficially, where most group-project planning actually happens.

Training & Placement Cell

Runs recruitment drives, resume reviews and interview prep out of the academic block year-round.

Applications run on the state engineering calendar.

I²IT is an autonomous college approved by AICTE and accredited by NAAC, affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University. Admission is through MHT-CET or JEE-Mains, alongside the regular Maharashtra engineering counselling rounds.
